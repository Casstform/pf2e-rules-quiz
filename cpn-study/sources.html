SOURCE NOTES

Built on the
right questions.

This independent question bank is a study aid for CPN(C) preparation. Its 184 questions are original paraphrases and scenarios, with one linked source per item. A source may define a competency while the answer applies it to a scenario; review the linked material and your current local policy if a point is uncertain.

Coverage

The six domain labels and approximate practice-session weights follow CNA's 2020 Perioperative Nursing Certification Exam Blueprint: ethical and professional practices 12.5%, safety 25%, infection prevention and control 22.5%, perioperative phases and anesthesia 20%, exceptional clinical events 12.5%, and managing resources 7.5%. These are midpoints of published ranges, not a prediction of any particular exam. CNA describes approximately 165 multiple-choice questions, with independent and case-based formats.

Our 184-question bank currently has 24 questions in six four-part cases. The weighted 20- and 50-question sessions aim for roughly one fifth case questions; smaller pools or category selections may vary. The bank is growing and is not a complete representation of every competency.

Questions are written for study, not patient-care orders. The site does not ingest, quote or claim to represent the unpublished ORNAC Standards. When you provide your copy, it can be reviewed and the bank expanded with newly authored questions and edition-specific citations.

Study method

A correct answer is recorded only once per attempt; missed and saved pools use this browser's local storage. Clearing browser data clears that progress. Options are shuffled for each session. No account or patient data is collected.
