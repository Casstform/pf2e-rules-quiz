SOURCE NOTES

Sources &
methods.

The 184 questions, explanations, and learning notes are independently written for CPN(C) study. Linked sources establish the principles; some clinical scenarios apply those principles rather than quote them. Review the current standard and local policy for patient care.

Coverage

The six domain labels and approximate exam weights follow CNA's 2020 Perioperative Nursing Certification Exam Blueprint: ethical and professional practices 12.5%, safety 25%, infection prevention and control 22.5%, perioperative phases and anesthesia 20%, exceptional clinical events 12.5%, and managing resources 7.5%. These are midpoints of published ranges, not a prediction of a particular exam. CNA describes approximately 165 multiple-choice questions, including independent and case-based formats.

The bank has 24 questions in six four-part cases. A mixed 20- or 50-question exam aims for roughly one fifth case questions; category selections may vary. The bank does not represent every competency.

This site does not contain ORNAC Standards or official exam questions. A future edition-specific expansion can use newly written questions when the standards are available.
